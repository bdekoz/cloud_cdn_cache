Improved Performance: 1-IX can enhance and optimize overall network performance through low-latency and direct network connections, resulting in improved efficiency.

Opportunities for Business Development:

1-IX provides ISPs and corporate clients with a versatile network environment to expand their network capabilities. By leveraging our network platform, you can grow and develop your business with the assurance of stable performance.

Additional Stability and Security:

1-IX will provide additional backup for current ISP channels (uplinks), thereby increasing the overall reliability of your network and minimizing the risk of network failures.
