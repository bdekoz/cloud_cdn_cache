New Data Center Now in Service！Another Stunning DC Member of Chindata Family

2020-01-17

On January 14th, Sangyuan Cloud Computing Campus, independently planned, invested, constructed, and operated by Chindata Group, was officially inaugurated in Huailai, Zhangjiakou. Chindata Group's in-depth next-generation layout of information technology zone in Rim Capital area winds along the Beijing-Zhangjiakou high-speed railway. With a larger footprint, Chindata helps Zhangjiakou to build a world-class hyper-scale digital infrastructure cluster, and to create a "China digital dam" from 1 to N.


" three points in one city” layout of Chindata Group in Huailai


The inauguration of the Capital Rim-Sangyuan Cloud Computing Industrial Campus denotes a perfected “three points in one city” deployment and a more completed capital rim layout in China. The Capital Rim· Guanting Lake Big Data Industrial Data Center Campus was put into operation in 2017, and the Central Capital · Cunrui Cloud Computing Industrial Base will soon be put into use. In the future, the “three points in one city” layout in Huailai, Zhangjiakou will support the hyper-scale computing requirement of the world's most demanding cloud computing industry customers.



Driven by the huge demand and good prospects for the development of the digital economy industry, Chindata Group, to further the "three points in one same city" of Zhangjiakou Huailai, has laid out Chindata's "next-generation information technology zone of capital rim". With Beijing, Hebei, and Shanxi as the core of matrix layout, it provides key customers with safe, reliable, scalable, cost-effective, and sustainable deployment of renewable energy business deployment scenarios. In 2020, Chindata Group is to realize the intensive of regional projects implementation, and will gradually establish a hyper-scale information technology infrastructure cluster around the capital with the characteristics of three streams of “energy flow, business flow, and data flow”. With the transformation and upgrading of traditional production capacity and the rapid development of the digital economy, it will gradually grow into one of the world's best data center location (benchmarking Greater Bay Area, NYU, US).


Chindata Group is the world's first hyper-scale digital real estate operator with pan-Asian emerging markets as its core business development area. In 2019, Chindata Group was shortlisted in the "Hurun Research Institute" Global Top 500 Unicorns, ranking the 1st among the Unicorn of global data center service providers. In the recent selection by the international authority "Green Peace Organization", Chindata Group was ranked No. 1 in the renewable energy performance of Chinese Internet technology companies.
