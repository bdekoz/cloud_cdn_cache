Overview

When using CLS, users can create logsets and log topics in different regions. A region refers to the geographical area of a physical data center, and networks between different regions are completely isolated. Users can select a nearby region based on their business scenarios and the geographical location of their target users to reduce access latency and improve access speed for log data.
