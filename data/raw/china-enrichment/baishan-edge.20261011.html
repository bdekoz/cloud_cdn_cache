Trusted & Chosen by Clients Across Industries
To expand into the Middle East, Northern Europe, and North Africa markets, a certain international game company needed localized high-performance game nodes to enhance user experience and reduce costs. Facing the issues of insufficient resource coverage and poor stability with traditional cloud service providers, Baishan Cloud deployed edge computing nodes in Dubai, Northern Europe, and other regions. It provided bare metal servers (BMS) to support high-performance computing, interconnected core computer rooms via dedicated lines, and integrated anti-DDoS protection capabilities. Ultimately, it helped the customer reduce the overall cost by over 30%; ensured terminal user access latency and operational stability, and improved the quality of localized services. With 7×24 operation and maintenance support and security protection capabilities, it effectively guaranteed the customer's business continuity.

Without a dedicated private network, this enterprise group had all its business systems exposed on the public internet, resulting in a broad attack surface. This imposed significant resource burdens during critical scenarios such as Classified Protection assessments and Network Protection Campaigns, yet yielded limited effectiveness. As subordinate branches proliferated, unplanned ICT asset deployment emerged, with resources dispersed across isolated networks ("networks outside networks") and unauthorized services, complicating network and security O&M.

To address these challenges, it implemented Baishan SD-WAN to build a globally centralized, unified enterprise private network. This enables centralized ICT asset planning and lays the groundwork for internal communication, IT resource sharing, and industrial IoT connectivity for lab equipment. Additionally, two convergence centers (Beijing and Shenzhen) serve as unified security gateways, eliminating direct external exposure for subordinate branches' services. This significantly enhances security posture and accelerates the development of a robust security architecture.
During its digital transformation journey, the enterprise faced challenges in building and transitioning between legacy and new systems, with a siloed architecture leading to inefficient collaboration. Leveraging its core capabilities in API management, orchestration, and open portals, Baishan Cloud constructed an enterprise-grade iPaaS integration foundation. This solution broke down system barriers and unlocked data value. The implementation has since achieved over 1,000 integrated interfaces with 99.99% service availability, laying a solid foundation for continuous digital advancement and further solidifying its market leadership.
