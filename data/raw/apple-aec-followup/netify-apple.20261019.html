Apple CDN

Apple CDN systems are deployed across 69 countries and territories on 8,957 IPs.

What Is Apple CDN?

Apple CDN - CDN
Apple CDN is a content delivery network that delivers Apple content to customers. It includes Apple Edge Cache (AEC) network equipment, which is Apple infrastructure deployed within ISP partner networks.
